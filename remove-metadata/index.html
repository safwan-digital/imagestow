Questions

What is photo metadata (EXIF)?

It is information stored inside the image file: camera or phone model, date and time, sometimes the app that edited it — and on many phone photos, the exact GPS location where it was taken.

Does removing metadata change my photo?

For JPGs, no — the tool cuts out only the hidden detail blocks and leaves the picture pixels exactly as they were. For PNG and WebP it makes a fresh copy of the picture, which carries no old details.

Why should I remove it before sharing?

A photo of something you are selling can reveal your home address through its GPS data. A clean copy shares the picture — nothing else.

Are my photos uploaded to be cleaned?

No. Cleaning happens in your browser. Sending your photo to a server to remove its location would defeat the point.
