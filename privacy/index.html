Privacy, in plain words

Your images are never uploaded. Every ImageStow tool runs inside your web browser, on your device. When you pick an image, your browser reads it, does the work in the page, and gives you the result to download. No copy of your image is sent to us or stored anywhere.

What does leave your device

Only program code, never your files:

  • The pages, design and tool code of this website itself.
  • On three tools only, a well-known open-source library is downloaded once so your browser can do the job: the HEIC reader (HEIC to JPG), the PDF maker (Images to PDF), and the ZIP maker (Bulk Compress and Favicon Generator). These libraries run on your device; they do not receive your images.

What we store

One small setting on your device: your light/dark theme choice, kept in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts and no sign-ups, and we show no advertising. The visit counters below do set cookies of their own, run by Google and Microsoft.

Counting visits

We use two well-known services — Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity — to count visits and see how this site is used: which pages and tools people open, what they click, how far they scroll, the kind of device and browser, and a rough location (country or city, never an exact address). We read those numbers to learn which tools are loved, which are confusing, and what to fix next.

Your images are never part of this. They are processed inside your browser and never uploaded, so there is nothing about them for anyone to track — no picture, no file name, nothing. The counters only ever see that a page was visited, never the files you work on.

Error reports

If a tool breaks in your browser, a service called Sentry sends us a short technical report about the error itself, so we can fix it: what kind of error happened, the page it happened on, and the kind of browser. Before anything is sent, the report is cleaned in your browser — web addresses are stripped of everything after the question mark, request headers and any user details are removed, and no personal data is sent. Your images are never part of an error report either — no picture, no file contents, no file name. Like the visit counters, error reporting only starts once you first interact with the page, or shortly after it has finished loading.

The ImageStow browser extension

The ImageStow extension for Chrome follows the same rule as this website. It converts and resizes images entirely inside your browser, on your device. It collects no personal information, shows no advertising and carries no analytics or tracking of any kind. The only things it keeps are two small preferences stored on your device — the last output format you chose and your light or dark theme. When you right-click an image and send it to ImageStow, the extension fetches that one image so it can work on it locally; the image is never sent to us or to anyone else.

When you close the tab

The images you were working on are gone with it. Your downloads are yours — check them before sharing, because a downloaded copy carries whatever you put in it.
