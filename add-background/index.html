Add Background / Border

Add a transparent PNG and ImageStow puts the colour you pick behind it, in your browser — the white product-photo look shops ask for. You can also add a coloured border of any width around any image. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to add background / border

  1. Add your image (PNG with transparency works best).
  2. Pick the background colour and set a border width if you want one.
  3. Press Download for the finished PNG.

Questions

Why does my PNG show a black background on some sites?

Some sites display transparency as black. Putting a real white background behind the picture fixes it everywhere.

Is it free?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Does it work on JPG photos too?

Yes — a JPG has no transparent areas, so the background colour only shows if you add a border around it.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
