SVG to PNG

Add an SVG logo or icon and ImageStow turns it into a PNG at the exact width you pick, in your browser. SVG scales cleanly, so the PNG comes out sharp at any size — handy for app icons, documents and sites that will not take SVG. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.
—

How to turn an SVG into a PNG

  1. Add your SVG file.
  2. Type the PNG width you need — the height follows automatically.
  3. Press Convert to PNG and download.

Questions

Is SVG to PNG free here?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

What size should I pick?

For a website logo, 512–1024 px wide is plenty. For print or app icons, go bigger — SVG stays sharp at any size.

Can I keep the transparent background?

Yes. Choose “Transparent” and the PNG keeps the see-through background. Choose white if the place you are uploading to shows black boxes instead.

My SVG has no size — what happens?

The tool reads the SVG's viewBox to work out its shape. If your file has neither a size nor a viewBox, add one in your SVG editor first.
