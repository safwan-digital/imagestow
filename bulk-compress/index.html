Bulk Compress Images

Free · No sign-up · Nothing uploaded

Add a whole set of images and ImageStow compresses them all in your browser, then packs them into one ZIP for a single download. No batch limit and no uploads — unlike popular compressors that cap free batches and send your photos to their servers.

Tap to choose images, or drop them here You can also paste an image (Ctrl+V), or drag one in from another tab. Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to compress many images at once

  1. Add all the images you want to compress.
  2. Set the quality — 80% suits most photos.
  3. Press Compress all and download the ZIP.

Questions

How many images can I compress at once?

As many as your device can handle — there is no batch limit here. Very large sets (hundreds of big photos) may take a while on a phone.

Is bulk compress free?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Every image is compressed in your browser and zipped on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

What format do the compressed images keep?

JPG stays JPG, WebP stays WebP, PNG stays PNG. JPG and WebP shrink the most; PNG photos shrink less because PNG has no quality setting.
