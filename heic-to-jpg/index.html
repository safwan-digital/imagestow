Questions

Is HEIC to JPG free here?

Yes, free with no account and no watermark.

Why can't my PC open HEIC photos?

HEIC is Apple's photo format. Many Windows programs and web forms do not support it yet; JPG works everywhere.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser. A small reader library is downloaded once so your browser can read HEIC — your photo itself never leaves your device.

Why did my HEIC file fail?

A few HEIC variants (some live photos and burst shots) cannot be read in a browser. Open the photo in the Photos app, share it as JPG, or take a screenshot and convert that instead.
