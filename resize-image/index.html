Resize Image

Add an image, type the new width and height — or just a percentage — and ImageStow resizes it in your browser. The lock keeps the shape correct so nothing looks stretched. Your image never leaves your device.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to resize image

  1. Add your image.
  2. Type a new width or height, or set a percentage.
  3. Press Resize and download.

Questions

Is resizing free here?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

What does the lock do?

It keeps the width and height in the same proportion as the original, so people and logos do not look stretched or squashed.

Can I make an image bigger?

Yes, but it will not get sharper — making an image bigger cannot add detail that was never there. It may look softer.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Resizing happens in your browser, on your device.
