Questions

Is it free to compress images here?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark, no daily limit.

Will my photo look worse?

At 80–90% quality most people cannot see a difference. Go lower only when a form has a strict size limit.

Are my images uploaded to be compressed?

No. Compression happens in your browser, on your device. That is also why it is instant.

Need an exact size, like under 200 KB?

Use our Compress to a Target Size tool — it adjusts everything for you until the file fits.
