Free · No sign-up · Nothing uploaded

Add an iPhone HEIC photo and ImageStow reads it, then traces it into an SVG in your browser — the picture is redrawn as shapes. A photo comes out as a flat, poster-style drawing, not a photo; simple pictures trace cleanly. Big photos can take a few seconds. Nothing is uploaded.

This tool traces your picture — it redraws it as shapes. Fewer colours give a cleaner, simpler SVG; more colours keep more detail. Logos, icons and flat graphics trace best; a photo becomes a flat, poster-style drawing. The tracing library loads once, only on this tool.

How to turn a HEIC photo into an SVG

  1. Add your HEIC photo from your iPhone.
  2. Set the colours — fewer gives a cleaner, simpler SVG; more keeps more detail.
  3. Press Convert to SVG and download.

Questions

Is HEIC to SVG free here?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Why does my photo look like a poster?

Tracing rebuilds a picture from a limited set of colours and shapes, and a photo holds millions of each. The SVG is a flat, poster-style drawing of your photo. If you need the photo itself as a normal picture, use HEIC to JPG instead.

Why did my HEIC file fail?

A few HEIC variants (some live photos and burst shots) cannot be read in a browser. Open the photo in the Photos app, share it as JPG, then use JPG to SVG on that copy.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The reading and tracing happen in your browser. A small reader library is downloaded once so your browser can read HEIC — your photo itself never leaves your device.
