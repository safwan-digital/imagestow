Questions

Why do websites give me WebP files?

WebP files are smaller, so sites serve them to load faster. Some apps and forms still cannot open them — JPG fixes that.

Is WebP to JPG free here?

Yes, completely free with no account and no watermark.

What happens to transparency?

JPG has no transparency, so see-through areas are filled with white.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
