Rotate & Flip

Photo came out sideways? Add it here, turn it left or right, flip it over if it is mirrored, and download the fixed copy — all in your browser, at full size. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here You can also paste an image (Ctrl+V), or drag one in from another tab. Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to rotate or flip an image

  1. Add your image.
  2. Use the rotate and flip buttons until it looks right.
  3. Press Download and save the fixed copy.

Questions

Is rotate and flip free?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

What is the difference between flip and rotate?

Rotate turns the whole picture like a steering wheel. Flip mirrors it — left becomes right (horizontal flip) or top becomes bottom (vertical flip).

Will the quality drop?

No. The picture is redrawn at its full original size and saved at high quality.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The fix happens in your browser on your device.
