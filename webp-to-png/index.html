WebP to PNG

Add a WebP image and ImageStow rewrites it as PNG in your browser. PNG keeps any transparent areas and works in every editor and office app. The file will usually be bigger than the WebP — that is normal. Your image never leaves your device.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to webp to png

  1. Add your WebP image.
  2. Press Convert to PNG.
  3. Download your PNG copy.

Questions

Is WebP to PNG free here?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Does it keep transparency?

Yes. Transparent areas in the WebP stay transparent in the PNG.

Why is the PNG bigger?

PNG stores pictures in a way that keeps every detail, which makes larger files than WebP. That is expected.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser on your device.
