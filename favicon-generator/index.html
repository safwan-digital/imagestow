How to favicon generator

  1. Add a square logo or image (the tool centre-crops if it is not square).
  2. Press Make favicons.
  3. Download the sizes you need, or the whole set as a ZIP.

Questions

What favicon sizes do I need?

32 px is the classic browser tab icon. 180 px is for Apple home screens, 192 and 512 are for Android and web apps, and 16/48 cover older browsers and Windows shortcuts. This tool makes all six.

Should my favicon image be square?

Yes — favicons are square. If your image is not square, the tool crops the middle square out of it, so keep your logo centred.

Is it free?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The icons are made in your browser.
