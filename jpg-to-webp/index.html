JPG to WebP

Drop in a JPG and ImageStow makes a WebP copy in your browser. WebP is the modern web format — at the same quality it is typically 25–35% smaller than JPG, so your pages load faster. Every current browser shows WebP. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to turn a JPG into WebP

  1. Add your JPG image.
  2. Set the quality — 85% is a good balance for websites.
  3. Press Convert to WebP and download.

Questions

Is JPG to WebP free here?

Yes. Free, no account, no watermark.

How much smaller is WebP?

Usually 25–35% smaller than the same photo as JPG at the same quality. Exact savings depend on the photo.

Does every browser support WebP?

Yes, all modern browsers — Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox — display WebP.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser; your photo never leaves your device.
