Crop Image

Free · No sign-up · Nothing uploaded

Add an image, drag a box over the part you want to keep, and ImageStow cuts away the rest in your browser — at full original quality. Move the box by dragging inside it, and resize it from the gold corner handle. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here You can also paste an image (Ctrl+V), or drag one in from another tab. Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

Drag on the picture to draw the box. Drag inside it to move it; drag the gold corner to resize it.

How to crop an image

  1. Add your image.
  2. Drag on the picture to draw the box; drag inside to move it, drag the corner to resize.
  3. Press Crop and download.

Questions

Is cropping free here?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. The kept area is copied from your original at full resolution — it is just smaller in size because it covers less of the picture.

Can I crop to an exact ratio, like a square?

Drag the box roughly square and check the pixel size shown after cropping — or use Resize for Social Media, which has exact platform sizes built in.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
