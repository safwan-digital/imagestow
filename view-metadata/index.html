View Photo Metadata

Add a photo and ImageStow reads the details hidden inside it — camera or phone model, when it was taken, and GPS location if present — and shows them in plain words, in your browser. The quickest way to check what a photo gives away before sharing. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here You can also paste an image (Ctrl+V), or drag one in from another tab. Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to view photo metadata

  1. Add your photo.
  2. Read the table — camera, date, and location if the photo carries them.
  3. If it reveals too much, use Remove Photo Metadata to get a clean copy.

Questions

What can this show me?

The file's name, size and type, the picture dimensions, and any EXIF details inside: camera maker and model, date taken, editing software, and GPS coordinates when present.

The tool says no hidden details found — is that possible?

Yes. Screenshots, images saved from most social apps, and photos that were already cleaned carry few or no hidden details. The tool still shows the file's basic facts, like its name and size.

Is it free?

Yes — free, no account.

Are my photos uploaded to be checked?

No. The reading happens in your browser. A photo you are checking for private details is never sent anywhere.
