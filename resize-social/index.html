  2. Pick the platform size you need.
  3. Choose Fill (crop) or Fit (whole picture), then download.

Questions

What size is an Instagram post?

1080 × 1080 px for a square post, 1080 × 1350 for portrait, and 1080 × 1920 for a story or reel cover. All three are in the list.

Fill or Fit — which should I choose?

Fill crops the edges so the picture covers the whole frame — best for photos. Fit keeps the whole picture and fills the gaps with a colour — best for logos and text you cannot crop.
