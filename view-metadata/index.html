How to view photo metadata

  1. Add your photo.
  2. Read the table — camera, date, and location if the photo carries them.
  3. If it reveals too much, use Remove Photo Metadata to get a clean copy.

Questions

What can this show me?

The file's name, size and type, the picture dimensions, and any EXIF details inside: camera maker and model, date taken, editing software, and GPS coordinates when present.

The tool says no metadata found — is that possible?

Yes. Screenshots, images saved from most social apps, and photos already cleaned carry little or nothing. The basic file facts are still shown.

Is it free?

Yes — free, no account.

Are my photos uploaded to be checked?

No. The reading happens in your browser. A photo you are checking for private details is never sent anywhere.
