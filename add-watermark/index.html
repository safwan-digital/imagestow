Add Watermark

Add a photo, type your name or shop name, and ImageStow stamps it onto the picture in your browser — you control the size, position and how see-through it is. Sellers use it so product photos are not copied and reposted as someone else's. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to add a watermark to a photo

  1. Add your photo.
  2. Type the watermark words and pick the size, position and how see-through it is.
  3. Press Add watermark and download.

Questions

Is adding a watermark free here?

Yes — free, no account, and the watermark is your own words, not ours.

Where is the best place for a watermark?

A corner is tidy; the middle is harder for others to crop out. A see-through level around 40–60% is readable without ruining the photo.

Can I watermark many photos at once?

This tool does one photo at a time so you can place the mark well. For many similar product photos, the same settings take seconds per photo.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The watermark is added in your browser; your photo never leaves your device.
