Blur Part of an Image

Drag a box over the part of a photo you need to hide — a face, a number plate, an address — and ImageStow blurs or pixelates just that area, in your browser. Hide as many areas as you like. The photo never leaves your device, which matters for a privacy job.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

Drag a box over the part to hide, then press the button below. Repeat for more areas.

How to blur part of an image

  1. Add your image.
  2. Drag a box over the part to hide, pick Blur or Pixelate, and press Hide this area. Repeat if needed.
  3. Press Download for the finished copy.

Questions

Blur or pixelate — which hides better?

Both work. Pixelate (big mosaic blocks) is the safer choice for text and number plates; blur looks softer for faces. Use a strong setting and make sure nothing is readable before you share.

Is it free?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Can I hide more than one area?

Yes. Hide one area, then drag another box and hide that too, as many times as you need.

Are my photos uploaded?

No — and for this tool that really matters. The original, unhidden photo never leaves your device.
