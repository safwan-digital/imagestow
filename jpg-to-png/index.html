JPG to PNG

Drop in a JPG and ImageStow rewrites it as a PNG right in your browser. The picture looks exactly the same; PNG just stores it in a different way, which suits logos, screenshots and anything that needs a transparent background later. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to turn a JPG into a PNG

  1. Add your JPG image.
  2. Press Convert to PNG.
  3. Download your PNG copy.

Questions

Is JPG to PNG free here?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account and no watermark.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The conversion happens in your browser, on your device. Your photo is never sent anywhere.

Will the PNG look better than the JPG?

No — converting cannot add detail back. The picture looks the same; the file is just stored as PNG, and it is often larger.

When should I use PNG instead of JPG?

For screenshots, logos, text and graphics with sharp edges, or when you need transparency. For photos, JPG is usually smaller.
