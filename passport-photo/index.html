Questions

What size is a passport photo?

The common standard is 35 mm wide × 45 mm tall. At print quality (300 dots per inch) that is 413 × 531 pixels, which is exactly what this tool makes.

Will this photo be accepted for my passport or visa?

Size is only one rule — lighting, background, glasses and head position rules differ by country. This tool gets the size and crop right; check your document's official photo rules before submitting.

Is it free?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Your photo is processed in your browser and never leaves your device — important for an ID photo.
