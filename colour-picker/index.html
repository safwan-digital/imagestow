Questions

Is the colour picker free?

Yes — free, no account.

How accurate is the colour?

It reads the actual pixel you clicked in the full-size image, so the code is exact for that spot. Photos have thousands of slightly different pixels — click a few spots to find the one you want.

What is the difference between HEX and RGB?

They are two ways of writing the same colour. HEX (like #C5A059) is what websites and design tools usually ask for; RGB lists the red, green and blue amounts.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The picking happens in your browser.
