About ImageStow

ImageStow is a free set of image tools that runs entirely in your browser: compress, resize, convert, edit and clean up images without uploading them anywhere.

It was built because the popular free tools all ask for the same thing — your photo, uploaded to their server, often with a batch limit or a sign-up wall. Your photos are personal. A picture of your ID, your product, your family should not need a trip to a stranger's server just to make it smaller.

The Stow family

ImageStow's sister site is PDFStow, free PDF tools built on the same rule: the work happens on your device, private by design.

Both are built by Safwan.Digital.
