PNG to WebP

Add a PNG and ImageStow converts it to WebP in your browser. WebP keeps the transparent background your PNG has, but the file is usually far smaller — ideal for logos and product shots on a website. Your image is never uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to turn a PNG into WebP

  1. Add your PNG image.
  2. Set the quality if you like.
  3. Press Convert to WebP and download.

Questions

Does WebP keep transparency?

Yes. Like PNG, WebP can store see-through areas, so logos and cut-outs stay clean.

Is PNG to WebP free here?

Yes — free, no account, no watermark.

How much smaller will it be?

Often 30–60% smaller than the PNG, depending on the picture. Screenshots with flat colours shrink the most.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, on your device.
