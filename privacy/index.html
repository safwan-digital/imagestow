Privacy, in plain words

Your images are never uploaded. Every ImageStow tool runs inside your web browser, on your device. When you pick an image, your browser reads it, does the work in the page, and gives you the result to download. No copy of your image is sent to us or stored anywhere.

What does leave your device

Only program code, never your files:

  • The pages, design and tool code of this website itself.
  • On three tools only, a well-known open-source library is downloaded once so your browser can do the job: the HEIC reader (HEIC to JPG), the PDF maker (Images to PDF), and the ZIP maker (Bulk Compress and Favicon Generator). These libraries run on your device; they do not receive your images.

What we store

One small setting on your device: your light/dark theme choice, kept in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts and no sign-ups. This site currently uses no analytics and no advertising, so there are no tracking cookies from us. If that ever changes, this page will say so plainly before it happens.

When you close the tab

The images you were working on are gone with it. Your downloads are yours — check them before sharing, because a downloaded copy carries whatever you put in it.
