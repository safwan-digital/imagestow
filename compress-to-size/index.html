Compress to a Target Size

Forms and portals reject photos over a size limit — often 200 KB. Pick your target and ImageStow keeps adjusting quality, then size, until the copy fits under it, in your browser. If a target cannot be reached without ruining the picture, it tells you honestly. Nothing is uploaded.

Tap to choose an image, or drop it here Your file stays on your device — it is never uploaded.

How to shrink an image to a target size

  1. Add your image.
  2. Pick 200 KB, 100 KB, 50 KB — or type your own limit.
  3. Press Compress to size and download the copy that fits.

Questions

How do I compress an image to 200 KB?

Add the image, choose the 200 KB target, and press the button. The tool lowers quality step by step — and shrinks the dimensions only if quality alone is not enough — until the file is under 200 KB.

Why did it shrink my picture's dimensions too?

Some photos cannot reach a small target by quality alone. When that happens the tool makes the picture slightly smaller as well, and tells you the new dimensions.

What if it says the target cannot be reached?

Very detailed photos have a floor below which they turn to mush. The tool gives you the smallest good copy it can make and says so — try a slightly higher target.

Are my images uploaded?

No. All the squeezing happens in your browser; your file never leaves your device.
